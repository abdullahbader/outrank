# 13 — Creative brief (prompt for designs and videos)

Copy the prompt below into a design or video tool (or give it to a designer). Bring the files back
in the formats listed at the end and they can go straight into the app.

---

**Prompt**

Create visual assets for **Outrank**, a mobile party trivia game for iOS and Android, in **Arabic
and English** (right-to-left and left-to-right).

**The game.** Friends sit together (about 10 players) with one host. Each question asks for a
ranked top 12 — for example "The 12 largest countries by area". Players take turns saying an
answer out loud (and can type it on their phone). The host confirms it. Points follow the rank:
ranks 1–9 give 1–9 points, rank 10 gives +20, but ranks 11 and 12 cost −11 and −12 (unless the
same player claims both 11 and 12 — a brave combo worth +20). Each player has two power cards:
**Wild** (×5 points on one answer, a joker card) and **Streak** (one extra answer, a flame). First
to 100 points wins; ties go to a tie-breaker. Turns have a countdown timer; there is a live
leaderboard, chat, emoji reactions and a winner celebration with confetti.

**Feel.** Social, competitive, playful but not childish; a premium party game, not a school quiz.
Fresh, warm and modern, made for game nights in Jordan and the Arab world, equally natural in
Arabic and English.

**Brand colours ("Modern Fresh").** Primary green `#3F8F65` · soft green `#DCEBDD` · muted gold
`#E8C66A` · muted blue `#75AFC5` · background `#F5F8F3` · cards `#FFFFFF` · text `#294638` ·
borders `#E3E9E1`. Accents: coral `#D4876F` for Wild, blue `#75AFC5` for Streak, muted red
`#C8614F` for ranks 11–12, gold for rank 10 and winners.

**Brand mark.** A 12-step ranking ladder: twelve columns stepping down from left to right; the
first nine in green/white, the 10th gold, the 11th and 12th red. A smart, cheerful boy mascot hops
from column to column.

**Style.** Soft 3D / clay-like illustration, rounded shapes, gentle lighting and shadows, clean
backgrounds, consistent across all pieces. No text baked into images (the app adds Arabic and
English text itself). No real brand logos or copyrighted characters.

**Please create:**

1. **Logo** — the ladder mark plus the word "Outrank" (English) and "أوت رانك" (Arabic), horizontal
   and stacked versions; also the mark alone.
2. **App icon** — 1024×1024, the ladder mark on a green-to-blue gradient; plus an Android adaptive
   icon foreground (mark only, centred in the inner 66%, transparent background).
3. **Splash screen** — mark centred on `#F5F8F3`, 1284×2778.
4. **Mascot** — the smart boy in 6 poses on transparent backgrounds: idle, jumping, celebrating
   (arms up), thinking, surprised, sad/oops. Also a girl mascot in the same poses.
5. **Category images** — 10 images, 1600×1000 landscape, each recognisable at a glance and
   readable with white text on top: Geography (world flags), History (Petra, Jordan), Sports
   (football stadium), Science (lab), Technology (circuits/devices), Entertainment (cinema),
   Food (Arabic mezze), Business (city skyline), Nature (Wadi Rum / wildlife), General Knowledge
   (library, light bulb).
6. **Power cards** — Wild (joker) and Streak (flame) as playing-card style artwork, 600×840,
   transparent background; plus a "used" version of each.
7. **Join art** — a character leaping onto a game controller, transparent, 512×512.
8. **Empty states** — 4 small illustrations (512×512, transparent): waiting for players, no games
   yet, no messages yet, offline.

**Videos (vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 15–30 s, no baked-in text or a separate text-free version):**

- **Intro / onboarding** — friends around a table, phones in hand, one shouts an answer, the
  leaderboard jumps, Wild and Streak cards fly in, the winner celebrates.
- **Winner celebration loop** — 4–6 s seamless loop: trophy, confetti, gold light, for the final
  results screen.
- **Power-up bursts** — two 2 s clips on transparent or green-screen background: Wild (joker card
  bursting with coral sparkles) and Streak (flame burst in blue).
- **Store trailer** — 30 s, horizontal 16:9 and vertical 9:16, showing the real gameplay screens.

**File formats:** PNG with transparency for illustrations and icons; SVG for the logo; MP4 (H.264)
under 8 MB each for videos; optional Lottie (.json) for small animations. Name files in English,
e.g. `category-history.png`, `mascot-boy-jump.png`, `video-intro-9x16.mp4`. Include the licence
or confirm the work is original and free to use in a commercial app.
